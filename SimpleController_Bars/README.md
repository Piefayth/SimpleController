# Simple Controller: Bars

1. Pick which bars you want in Options > AddOns > Simple Controller: Bars.
2. After reloading UI after picking, you can also reposition them with the sliders in Options > AddOns > Simple Controller: Bars Layout.
    2b. Custom 1-4 are bars on a chord of your own (a paddle or a stick click, with any shoulders and triggers): press "Set Chord", then hold and release the combination.
3. Check "Extra Buttons" if you want to enable additional non-chorded keypresses (for example, I bind L3 to my shapeshift macro instead of autorun thorugh this)
    3b. These extra buttons are bindable in the normal Blizzard keybindings settings
5. Bindings - Bind as normal through the spellbook, macro book, bag, whatever. Tap square (or your platform's equivalent) on the binding screen to enable binding to the extra bars
