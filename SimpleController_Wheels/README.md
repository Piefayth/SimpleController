# Simple Controller: Wheels

**Use a wheel:** press its button on your bars. Push the right stick toward a slot, let go. Circle: close.

**Make a wheel:** in the spellbook, Triangle (Edit Action Bar), then Cross (Add Wheel). Hold a bar's buttons, press where the wheel goes.

**Put a spell on a wheel:** in the spellbook, Square on the spell, then Cross (Place on wheel). Hold the wheel's bar buttons, press the wheel. Push the right stick toward a slot, let go.

**Four or eight slots:** Options > AddOns > Simple Controller: Wheels.

**Its button again while open** (nothing, cast the last one, or close): Options > AddOns > Simple Controller: Wheels.

**Lost a wheel:** it is in your macros, named "Wheel 1", "Wheel 2"...
